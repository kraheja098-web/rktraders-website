Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our greaseproof butter paper sheets and custom printing service.

What is the difference between butter paper, parchment paper, and OGR greaseproof paper?

Direct Answer: RK Traders greaseproof butter paper is made of high-density virgin wood pulp with natural Oil and Grease Resistant (OGR) mechanical fibers. Unlike cheap wax paper coated with petroleum paraffin that melts, our OGR sheets withstand heat up to 220°C (428°F) without burning or smoking, making them safe for ovens, air fryers, and hot food wrapping.

  • Butter Paper (OGR): High-density fiber barrier for greasy food wrapping, basket liners, and baking.
  • Wax Paper: Coated in paraffin wax; melts at warm temperatures and is strictly not oven-safe.
  • Parchment Paper: Silicone-coated release paper primarily used for industrial non-stick baking.
What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for custom logo printed butter paper?

Direct Answer: RK Traders provides an industry-leading low Minimum Order Quantity starting at just 5,000 sheets for single-color and multi-color custom logo printing. We provide free digital layout proofs and fast delivery across India.

Are the printing inks and paper food-safe, PFAS-free, and odorless?

Direct Answer: Yes, 100%. Our sheets comply with FSSAI and US FDA 21 CFR 176.170 direct food contact standards. They are 100% PFAS-free, chlorine-free, and printed exclusively with heavy-metal-free edible vegetable soy inks that never bleed or leave chemical odors.

Can these greaseproof sheets be used in commercial baking ovens and air fryers?

Direct Answer: Yes. Our butter paper sheets are heat-rated up to 220°C (428°F). They are thoroughly tested and safe for commercial convection ovens, air fryers, and microwave warming without scorching or curling.

How quickly can plain sheets and physical trial sample kits be dispatched?

Direct Answer: Standard pre-cut plain sheets (8x8, 10x10, 11x11, 12x12, 10x14) and physical sample trial kits dispatch from our Panipat warehouse within 24 to 48 hours. Custom printed orders dispatch within 7 to 10 working days following artwork approval.
